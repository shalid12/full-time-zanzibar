Day by day

Your suggested
itinerary

Day 1: Spice Farm & Prison Island
Begin with an immersive spice farm tour. Discover the island's rich agricultural heritage as you explore plantations of cloves, cinnamon, vanilla, and other spices that made Zanzibar famous. Learn how these spices are grown, harvested, and used in local cuisine and traditional medicine. Taste fresh spices and tropical fruits directly from the farm. In the afternoon, take a boat to Prison Island (Changuu Island), also known as Changuu Island. This historic island was once used as a prison for rebellious slaves, but today it's famous for its giant Aldabra tortoises, brought from the Seychelles in the late 19th century. Walk among these gentle giants, some of which are over than 100 years old, and learn about their fascinating history. Enjoy the island's beautiful beaches andmarine life before returning to your hotel.

Day 2: Stone Town with Shopping
Explore the historic Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the cultural heart of Zanzibar. Wander through the narrow, winding alleys lined with ancient Arabic houses and decorative doors. Visit iconic landmarks including the Old Fort, built by the Omani Arabs in the 17th century, and the House of Wonders, one of the finest examples of Swahili architecture. Discover the fascinating history of the Slave Church and the Memorial Cathedral, built on the site of the former slave market. Explore the vibrant local markets, where you can find everything from fresh spices and textiles to handcrafted souvenirs. Enjoy the opportunity to shop for unique gifts and mementos. Your 2-day Zanzibar adventure concludes with lasting memories of the Spice Island.
