Useful answers

Rental FAQs

Confirm vehicle-specific conditions with our team before payment.

Are the displayed prices final?

They are starting daily rates. Your final quote depends on dates, rental length, vehicle availability, delivery location and agreed rental terms.

Can the vehicle be delivered to my hotel?

Delivery may be arranged depending on your hotel location, timing and selected vehicle. Any delivery or collection charge will be confirmed in advance.

Do I need a Zanzibar driving permit?

A local permit may be required in addition to your valid driving license. Send a copy of your license so the team can explain the current requirement for your booking.

What insurance is included?

Insurance conditions, excess liability and exclusions vary. Review the written terms carefully and ask questions before accepting the vehicle.

Can I rent for several days?

Yes. Multi-day arrangements are available, subject to the selected vehicle and your dates. Ask whether a longer-rental rate applies.

What should I check at handover?

Inspect the exterior, tires, fuel level, lights and controls. Photograph the condition and ensure existing marks are recorded in the agreement.
