Safari overview

About this
safari

Embark on a two-day adventure from Zanzibar to two of Tanzania’s most iconic safari destinations: Tarangire National Park and the world-famous Ngorongoro Crater. Tarangire is celebrated for its vast elephant herds, towering baobab trees, and sweeping savannahs, offering guests an authentic taste of Africa’s wilderness.

Ngorongoro, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is often referred to as the “Eighth Wonder of the World.” Its dramatic crater landscape is home to an incredible concentration of wildlife, including lions, zebras, wildebeests, elephants, and the endangered black rhino. Together, these parks provide a perfect blend of breathtaking scenery and unforgettable safari encounters.

Suggested itinerary

How the experience
usually unfolds

The adventure begins with an early morning transfer from Zanzibar, including a short domestic flight to mainland Tanzania. Upon arrival, guests are welcomed by professional safari guides and driven to Tarangire National Park. The first day is spent on thrilling game drives across the park’s vast plains, spotting elephants, giraffes, zebras, and predators. In the evening, guests enjoy dinner and an overnight stay at a safari lodge or camp near the park.

On the second day, the journey continues to Ngorongoro Crater. Guests descend into the crater floor for a morning game drive, where wildlife roams freely across open grasslands and marshes. Highlights include lions, wildebeests, zebras, elephants, and possibly the rare black rhino. After lunch at a scenic picnic site, the safari concludes with a return transfer back to Zanzibar, leaving guests with unforgettable memories of Tanzania’s natural wonders.

Can this safari start from Zanzibar?

Yes. Our team can coordinate the connections between Zanzibar and the mainland as part of your confirmed itinerary.

Are wildlife sightings guaranteed?

No. Wildlife moves freely in natural habitats, although experienced guides use local knowledge to maximize viewing opportunities.

What should I pack?

Bring travel documents, sun protection, comfortable neutral clothing, closed shoes, personal medication and a light layer for early mornings.
