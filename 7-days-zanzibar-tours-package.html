Day by day

Your suggested
itinerary

Day 1: From Airport to Hotel
Welcome to Zanzibar! Upon arrival at Zanzibar International Airport, you will be greeted by our professional driver and transferred to your hotel. Enjoy a smooth and comfortable transfer to your accommodation. Take the rest of the day to settle in, relax by the pool or beach, and acclimate to the island atmosphere. Your 7-day Zanzibar adventure begins!

Day 2: Spice Farm, Prison Island & Stone Town
Start with an immersive spice farm tour. Discover the island's rich agricultural heritage as you explore plantations of cloves, cinnamon, vanilla, and other spices that made Zanzibar famous. Taste fresh spices and tropical fruits directly from the farm. Next, take a boat to Prison Island (Changuu Island), known for its giant tortoises and historical significance. Explore the island's history and enjoy the company of these gentle giants. In the afternoon, explore historic Stone Town, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Wander through winding alleys, visit the Old Fort, House of Wonders, and the famous Slave Church. Discover the rich cultural heritage of Zanzibar.

Day 3: Dolphin Tour, Aquarium & Sunset Dhow Cruise
Embark on an exciting dolphin watching tour to spot playful dolphins in their natural habitat. Have the incredible opportunity to swim and snorkel alongside these magnificent creatures in the warm Indian Ocean. After the dolphin adventure, visit the Nungwi Aquarium to learn about marine life and perhaps swim with turtles. In the evening, experience the magical sunset dhow cruise. Sail aboard a traditional wooden dhow as the sun dips below the horizon, painting the sky in shades of orange and pink. Enjoy refreshments and the serene atmosphere of the Indian Ocean at sunset.

Day 4: Relaxing in Hotel
Take a well-deserved break and enjoy a full day of relaxation at your hotel or one of Zanzibar's beautiful beaches. Unwind by the pool or beach, soak up the sun, and enjoy the tranquil island atmosphere. Indulge in spa treatments, read a book, or simply savor the stunning views. This day is designed for you to recharge and enjoy the laid-back island lifestyle at your own pace.

Day 5: Safari Blue
Experience the famous Safari Blue tour, one of Zanzibar's most popular excursions. This full-day adventure takes you to some of the island's most stunning locations. Visit the beautiful mangroves, explore hidden caves, and enjoy swimming in crystal-clear waters. Enjoy a seafood lunch on a pristine sandbank or beach. The Safari Blue tour offers a perfect combination of adventure, nature, and relaxation, showcasing the natural beauty of Zanzibar's coastline.

Day 6: Jozani Forest & Mtende Beach
Journey to Jozani Forest, the largest unspoiled mangrove forest in Zanzibar. Embark on a guided walk through this lush paradise and discover the rare red colobus monkeys found only in Zanzibar. Learn about the forest's diverse ecosystem, medicinal plants, and unique wildlife. In the afternoon, head to the beautiful Mtende Beach, one of Zanzibar's most pristine and secluded beaches. Enjoy swimming, sunbathing, and relaxing on the white sandy beach surrounded by turquoise waters. Experience the tranquility of this hidden gem away from the crowds.

Day 7: Hotel to Airport
On your final day, enjoy a comfortable and convenient transfer from your hotel to Zanzibar International Airport. Depending on your flight time, you may have the opportunity for some last-minute shopping or sightseeing in Stone Town before your departure. Our professional driver will ensure you arrive at the airport on time for your departure, marking the end of your unforgettable 7-day Zanzibar adventure.
