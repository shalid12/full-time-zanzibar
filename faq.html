Planning & booking

How do I book an experience?

Contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or the contact form. Share your preferred date, group size, hotel and interests so we can confirm suitable arrangements.

Can I customize an itinerary?

Yes. Private tours and multi-day plans can be adapted around your available time, travel pace and preferred experiences.

How early should I book?

Booking early is recommended for the best choice of dates. Last-minute requests are welcome and depend on guide, vehicle and activity availability.

What information should I send?

Please include your travel date, number of guests, pickup location and the places or activities you are interested in.

Tours & guides

Are the tours private?

Our featured experiences are designed as private tours, allowing greater comfort, attention and flexibility for your group.

What languages are available?

Tell us your preferred language when enquiring. Guide availability varies by date and should be confirmed in advance.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, sun protection, water-friendly footwear and a camera are useful. Activity-specific guidance is shared before your tour.

Can plans change because of weather?

Ocean and outdoor activities may be adjusted when conditions affect safety or comfort. Our team will explain suitable alternatives.

Transport & pickup

Do you provide airport pickup?

Yes. Private transfers can be arranged from Zanzibar Airport or the ferry terminal to hotels across the island.

Can you collect us from our hotel?

Hotel pickup can be coordinated for tours and transfers. Send the hotel name and area so the meeting details can be confirmed.

Do you have vehicles for groups?

Vehicle options are available for couples, families and larger groups. Group size helps us select an appropriate vehicle.

Tanzania safaris

Can I take a safari from Zanzibar?

Yes. We coordinate day trips and multi-day safari journeys linking Zanzibar with several mainland national parks.

Which parks can I visit?

Options include Mikumi, Nyerere, Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire and Lake Manyara, depending on your available time and route.

Are flights and transfers coordinated?

Relevant connections can be included in a safari plan. Exact inclusions are listed clearly in the itinerary provided for your trip.

Families & groups

Are experiences suitable for children?

Many experiences work well for families. Share the children’s ages so we can suggest suitable timing and activities.

Can you arrange student or business groups?

Yes. Group transport, educational visits and coordinated itineraries can be discussed according to group needs.

Can dietary needs be considered?

Please share dietary requirements before the trip. We will communicate them where meals are part of the planned experience.
