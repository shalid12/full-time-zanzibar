About this

Immerse yourself in the flavors and traditions of Zanzibar with a full-day cultural experience designed for food lovers and explorers alike. From selecting fresh ingredients alongside local vendors to preparing authentic Swahili dishes under expert guidance, every step of the journey is interactive and enriching. Perfect for travelers seeking cultural immersion, this experience blends culinary discovery with warm hospitality, leaving guests with both new skills and unforgettable memories.

Suggested itinerary

How the experience
usually unfolds

The day begins with a guided walk through a lively local market, where guests explore colorful stalls filled with spices, fruits, and vegetables. Your guide explains the origins and uses of Zanzibar’s famous spices while helping select fresh ingredients for the cooking class.

Next, guests are welcomed into a traditional kitchen setting, where they learn step-by-step how to prepare classic Swahili dishes. Under the guidance of local chefs, you’ll cook with aromatic spices and fresh produce, creating a meal that reflects Zanzibar’s rich culinary heritage. The experience concludes with everyone sharing the dishes together, celebrating culture, flavor, and community.
