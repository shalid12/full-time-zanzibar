Tour overview

About this
experience

Embark on a full-day journey that blends cultural immersion with coastal charm. Begin with a visit to a traditional Massai village, where guests experience the vibrant customs, dances, and daily life of one of Africa’s most iconic tribes. This unique encounter offers insight into the Massai’s traditions, resilience, and heritage.

The adventure continues with a magical sunset dhow cruise along Zanzibar’s coastline. As the sun dips below the horizon, guests sail aboard a traditional wooden dhow, enjoying the tranquil rhythm of the ocean and breathtaking views. This tour is perfect for travelers seeking both cultural discovery and romantic relaxation.

Suggested itinerary

How the experience
usually unfolds

The day begins with a guided transfer to a Massai village, where guests are warmly welcomed by the community. You’ll learn about the tribe’s history, traditions, and daily practices, with opportunities to engage in cultural activities and witness traditional dances.

After the village experience, the journey continues to Zanzibar’s coast for a sunset dhow cruise. Guests board a traditional wooden dhow and sail across turquoise waters, enjoying the peaceful atmosphere as the sun sets over the horizon. The tour concludes with a return to shore, leaving guests with lasting memories of culture and coastal beauty.
