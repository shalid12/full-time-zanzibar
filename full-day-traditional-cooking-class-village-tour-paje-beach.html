About this

Immerse yourself in the authentic culture of Zanzibar with a full-day experience that blends culinary discovery, village exploration, and coastal relaxation. This tour is designed for travelers who want to connect with local traditions while enjoying the island’s natural beauty.

Suggested itinerary

How the experience
usually unfolds

The day begins with a guided visit to a traditional Zanzibar village, where guests are welcomed by locals and introduced to daily life, customs, and community activities. You’ll explore homes, farms, and workshops, gaining insight into the island’s heritage.

Next, guests participate in a traditional cooking class, selecting fresh ingredients and learning how to prepare authentic Swahili dishes under the guidance of local chefs. After enjoying the meal together, the journey continues to Paje Beach, where guests relax on powdery white sands, swim in turquoise waters, and soak in the vibrant atmosphere. The tour concludes with a return transfer, leaving guests with lasting memories of Zanzibar’s culture and coastline.
