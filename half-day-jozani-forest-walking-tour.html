Tour overview

About this
experience

Discover the untouched natural beauty of Zanzibar on our Half-Day Jozani Forest Walking Tour, an unforgettable journey through the island's last remaining indigenous forest. This carefully curated nature experience takes you deep into the heart of the Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park, where you'll encounter pristine mangroves, towering ebony trees, and incredible biodiversity that has flourished for centuries. As you walk along the well-maintained trails, your expert guide will introduce you to the forest's fascinating ecosystem, pointing out rare plant species and the incredible array of wildlife thatcall this protected area home.

The highlight of every tour is undoubtedly the opportunity to observe the endangered Red Colobus monkeys in their natural habitat – a species found nowhere else on Earth. These playful primates, unique to Zanzibar, have adapted remarkably to forest life, and watching them swing through the canopy is a truly magical experience. Our knowledgeable guides are passionate about conservation and will share fascinating insights about the forest's ecology, the efforts to protect endangered species, and Swahili culture. Whether you're a nature enthusiast, photography lover, or simply seeking a break from the beach, this walking tour offers an authentic connection with Zanzibar's natural heritage that few visitors ever experience.

How the experience
usually unfolds

Your adventure begins at the entrance of Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park, where your knowledgeable guide will greet you and provide a fascinating introduction to the forest's ecology and history. As you venture into the forest along the well-maintained walking trails, you'll be surrounded by towering trees, diverse plant species, and the sounds of nature. The first part of your journey takes you through the indigenous high forest, where sunlight filters through the dense canopy, creating a magical atmosphere unlike anywhere else on Zanzibar.

The tour continues to the mangrove areas, where you'll explore the unique tidal creek system and learn about the crucial role mangroves play in protecting Zanzibar's coastline. Here, you may spot various bird species, crabs, and other marine life that thrive in this brackish environment. The highlight of every tour is the visit to the Red Colobus monkey viewing area, where these endangered primates are often seen feeding, playing, and swinging through the trees. Your guide will share fascinating facts about their behavior and the conservation efforts to protect this endemic species. The tour concludes at the park entrance, where you can purchase locally made crafts and souvenirs before your return transfer.
