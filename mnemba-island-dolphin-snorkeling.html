Tour overview

A memorable morning
on the Indian Ocean

Experience the beauty of Zanzibar’s north-east coast on a half-day Mnemba Island dolphin-watching and snorkeling adventure. Travel by traditional dhow or speedboat toward the waters surrounding Mnemba Atoll, an area known for clear seas, coral reefs and varied marine life.

Your experienced crew will look for wild dolphins while maintaining a respectful distance and responding to conditions on the day. When conditions are suitable, continue to a snorkeling area where colorful reef fish, coral formations and occasional sea turtles may be seen. The experience combines ocean adventure, time in the water and a relaxed boat journey through Zanzibar’s tropical seascape.

Responsible wildlife experience

Dolphins and turtles are wild animals, so sightings and opportunities to enter the water cannot be guaranteed. Our crew prioritizes guest safety, animal welfare and respectful observation.

Suggested itinerary

How your morning
usually unfolds

Exact timing and the order of activities may change with tides, weather and wildlife movement.

01 Meet your crew and board

Arrive at the departure beach for a short welcome, safety briefing and introduction before boarding your dhow or speedboat.
02 Search for wild dolphins

Cruise through the coastal waters while the crew looks for dolphin pods. Any encounter is approached calmly and respectfully.
03 Snorkel near Mnemba Atoll

At a suitable reef location, use the provided mask, snorkel and fins to discover colorful fish, coral formations and other marine life.
04 Refresh and return

Enjoy water and light refreshments, then relax during the scenic boat journey back to the departure point.

Good to know

Frequently asked
questions

Are dolphin sightings guaranteed?

No. Dolphins are wild animals and move freely. The crew uses local knowledge to look for them, but no responsible operator can guarantee a sighting.

Do I need snorkeling experience?

No previous experience is required, but guests should be comfortable around water. Tell the team your confidence level so the crew can provide suitable support.

Is hotel transfer included?

Hotel pickup and return are not included in the standard tour unless stated in your confirmed quote. We can arrange a private transfer from most Zanzibar hotels.

What happens if the weather is unsuitable?

Ocean safety comes first. The route, departure time or date may be adjusted if wind, waves or other conditions make the planned experience unsuitable.

Can children join the tour?

Families are welcome, subject to the day’s sea conditions and appropriate adult supervision. Share the children’s ages when requesting your quote.
